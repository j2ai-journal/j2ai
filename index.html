About the journal

Publishing AI that has left the lab.

Journal of Artificial Intelligence Applications and Impact publishes original research, reviews, and case studies on the design, deployment, and evaluation of artificial intelligence systems across scientific, industrial, and societal domains. We prioritize work that demonstrates how AI techniques are deployed, assessed, and evaluated for effect in practice — technical, economic, social, or ethical — over purely theoretical contributions.

For authors

What to know before you submit.

Manuscript files
Word or LaTeX, anonymized for double-blind review — remove author names, affiliations, and identifying references from the body text.
Reference style
IEEE or APA formatting accepted.
Disclosures
Conflict of interest, data availability, and any use of generative AI tools in manuscript preparation must be disclosed.
Ethics
J2AI follows COPE guidelines on authorship, plagiarism screening, and corrections or retractions.
